## Priority Issues
- [P0] Enquiry is a fragile mailto with no validation, feedback or fallback (L263-268, 985-990). Fix: WhatsApp fallback, require phone/email, trim form, ideally real submit with success panel. Commands: harden, clarify.
- [P1] Parent-deciding facts missing; differentiator buried (Pretoria, CAPS/IEB/Cambridge, price, response time, ADHD/learning difficulties). Commands: clarify.
- [P1] Middle of page repetitive; strongest material late ("school readiness to Grade 12" ~8x, "4+ years" 4x). Commands: distill, layout.
- [P1] Tutor recruitment pollutes parent journey ("Apply" in nav; recruitment band is last thing on the page, L1010-1037). Commands: distill.
- [P2] Contrast/legibility defects: Why-card text on translucent white (~3.9:1 est.), tan input borders (~1.3:1), outline-none inputs, FAQ "+" white on mint (~2.2:1), 8-10px micro-text, footer contact not tappable. Commands: audit, colorize, polish.
