## Platform

web

## Users

Parents of school-age children (school-readiness through Grade 12) in South Africa, seeking a tutor for their child. Sessions are available online (any location) or in person for families in the Pretoria area. Students themselves are a secondary audience (e.g. older students seeking exam prep).

## Product Purpose

Bloom Tutoring provides 1-on-1 personalized tutoring across all core subjects, from school-readiness through Grade 12, with a specialization in Math up to AP level. The goal is to move students from feeling overwhelmed to feeling confident, addressing both academic gaps and the student's relationship to learning itself — not just grades.

## Positioning

Founded on the founder's own experience of being a misunderstood student with ADHD, Bloom Tutoring positions itself around truly personalized, whole-child support rather than one-size-fits-all worksheets or drilling — matching tutors to each child's learning style, subject needs, and personality, and adjusting the match whenever it isn't working.

## Operating Context

- Curricula: CAPS, IEB, and Cambridge (South African and international school systems).
- Session format: online (any location) or in-person for the Pretoria area.
- Typical cadence: most families start weekly, adjusting frequency around goals and exams.
- Enquiry flow: parents submit a short form (name, child's grade, message) that opens a pre-filled mailto to Bloomtutoringadmin@gmail.com; direct email and phone (068 241 4458) are also given.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Services: Early Learners / school readiness, all core subjects Grade 00–12, Math specialization through AP, exam prep, ongoing homework help.
- Tutors are described as experienced and hand-picked; team is growing beyond the two founders.

- No booking/scheduling system yet — enquiries go through the contact form/email/phone, not self-service booking.

## Product Principles

- Meet every child where they are — no student is "too behind" or "too advanced."
- Whole-child focus: change how the student feels about learning, not just the grade.
- Personalization over standardization — every plan built around how the child actually learns, no cookie-cutter worksheets.
- Human credibility first: the founder's own lived experience (ADHD, being misunderstood) and the co-founder's specialist credentials are the trust foundation, ahead of scale or marketing claims.
- Flexible fit: tutor matches are adjustable anytime the fit isn't right, and both online and in-person (Pretoria) formats are offered to meet families where they are.
